# 📦 E-commerce Logistics Profit Simulation

## 🎯 Business Problem

In e-commerce logistics operations, fluctuations in fuel costs directly impact delivery expenses and overall profitability.

This project aims to answer:
👉 How should companies adjust pricing and cost strategies to maintain profitability under changing fuel prices?

## 📊 Analysis Objectives
．Evaluate the impact of fuel cost changes on logistics profitability
．Analyze key cost components (fuel, labor, delivery efficiency)
．Perform scenario analysis under different fuel price conditions
．Provide actionable business recommendations

## 🧰 Tools & Methods
．Python (data processing and simulation)
．Basic cost-profit modeling
．Scenario analysis
．Data visualization

## 🔍 Key Findings
．Fuel cost is the most significant variable affecting logistics expenses
．Profitability declines noticeably as fuel prices increase
．Delivery efficiency (cost per order) amplifies the impact on margins
．Fixed costs become more dominant under high-cost scenarios, reducing profit flexibility

## 💡 Business Insights
．Adjust delivery fees or minimum order thresholds when fuel costs rise
．Improve delivery efficiency (e.g., route optimization) to offset cost pressure
．Apply differentiated pricing strategies for high-cost regions
．Leverage promotions during low fuel cost periods to increase order volume and maximize profit

## 📈 Conclusion

This project highlights that fuel cost and delivery efficiency are critical drivers of logistics profitability.
Through scenario analysis, businesses can proactively adjust pricing and operational strategies to mitigate risks from cost fluctuations.
