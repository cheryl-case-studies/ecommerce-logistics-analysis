# E-commerce Logistics Profit Simulation  
## Business Analysis Report

---

## 1. Business Problem

In e-commerce logistics operations, fluctuations in fuel costs directly impact delivery expenses and overall profitability.

This project explores how companies can adjust pricing and cost strategies to maintain profitability under changing fuel prices.

---

## 2. Objectives

- Evaluate the impact of fuel cost changes on profitability  
- Analyze key cost components (fuel, labor, delivery efficiency)  
- Perform scenario analysis under different fuel price conditions  
- Provide business recommendations  

---

## 3. Methodology

- Data processing using Python  
- Cost-profit simulation modeling  
- Scenario analysis  
- Data visualization  

---

## 4. Key Findings

- Fuel cost is the most significant factor affecting logistics costs  
- Profit decreases as fuel prices increase  
- Delivery efficiency amplifies the impact on profitability  
- Fixed costs reduce flexibility under high-cost scenarios  

---

## 5. Business Insights

- Adjust delivery fees or minimum order thresholds when fuel costs rise  
- Improve delivery efficiency (e.g., route optimization)  
- Apply differentiated pricing strategies for high-cost regions  
- Increase promotional efforts during low fuel cost periods  

---

## 6. Conclusion

Fuel cost and delivery efficiency are the key drivers of logistics profitability.

Through scenario analysis, businesses can proactively adjust pricing and operational strategies to reduce risks from cost fluctuations.
